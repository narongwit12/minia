Reading the three

Option 01 is the quietest and most timeless — but the dot is generic as a standalone symbol; it depends on the wordmark being present.

Option 02 owns the strongest link to the product — the mark is the actual door —. Its risk: vertical slots read "on/off switch" to some eyes at tiny sizes.

Option 03 now carries the richest meaning: half arrived, half still to come, rising from the ground. It is the only mark that stands fully alone as a symbol — and engraves in two depths: solid etch above, fine line below.

Production: outline all type; etch-test all three lids on Fameline offcuts; register the chosen mark + wordmark together at DIP.
