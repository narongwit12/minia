The road to opening

The first house
meets its first guests.

The house is nearly complete. What happens next decides how Mini A enters the world. This is the master plan for the private opening — every workstream from the exhibition guests walk through before touching the house, to the card that lands in their hands, to the story the press tells the morning after.

Target window · Q4 2026 Fameline Office Lawn, Bangkok 60–80 invited executives Private, invitation-only evening First batch of exclusive customers
The organizing idea01

One continuous journey, not an open house.

Guests never walk straight into the product. They pass through a staged exhibition that builds conviction — so by the time the doors open, the house is a conclusion, not an introduction.

01

Valet, registration, welcome drink. Guest receives the Mini A Passport.

Exhibition content02

Five zones before the front door.

A temporary pavilion / covered walk on the lawn, sequenced so each zone answers the next natural question: Why does this exist? → How is it made? → What is it made of? → How precise is it? → How does it live with me?

A Zone A · Manifesto

The Story Wall

A typographic wall stating the thesis: housing re-imagined as a manufactured product. Vision, the A49 × Fameline partnership, and the problem with conventional construction — told in three panels.

Produce — copywriting (TH/EN), large-format print, wall lighting.
B Zone B · Process

The Factory Film

A dark micro-theatre looping the 6–8 min documentary: robotic fabrication, QC gates, module assembly, the drive from factory to this exact lawn. Ends on a timelapse of the house landing on site.

Produce — film edit, seating for 12, sound isolation, projection.
C Zone C · Material

Material Library

Touchable samples: wall composites, glazing sections, floor systems, joinery. Each labelled with durability and maintenance data — the 10-year no-maintenance promise made physical.

Produce — sample plinths, spec cards, ambient light.
D Zone D · Precision

The Cutaway

A 1:1 open module section showing structure, insulation, services routing, and connection details. Tolerance figures displayed beside automotive comparisons.

Produce — display module from factory, annotation graphics.
E Zone E · Smart living

Living Intelligence

Interactive station previewing the aIoT layer: presence, climate preconditioning, security, energy view. A live feed from the actual house primes what guests are about to experience inside.

Produce — demo app build, screen station, live house link.

Rules for every zone

Invitation system04

The invitation is the first product they touch.

Physical format

A material object, not a card

A slim rigid box, matte black, blind-debossed MINI A. Inside: a letterpress invitation card, a genuine offcut of the house's wall material engraved with the guest's name and edition number, and a single line of purpose.

"You are invited to be among the first to stand inside the future of living. One evening. One house. Eighty guests." — personalized, hand-delivered by courier to office or home
Numbered 001–080 Material sample doubles as keepsake QR → private RSVP page No brand explanation — mystery invites curiosity

PR strategy05

Whisper. Reveal. Echo.

The event is private, but its story is public. PR runs in three phases around the evening — scarcity before, spectacle during, momentum after.

Phase 1 · Before

Whisper

  • Teaser site + cryptic social presence (no product shots)
  • One anchor magazine granted an exclusive embargoed feature
  • Factory BTS clips seeded to architecture community
  • KOL scouting — 3–5 design/lifestyle creators signed
Phase 2 · Event week

Reveal

  • Press preview day — journalists tour before guests
  • Embargoed press kit: release, specs, imagery, founder bios
  • Embargo lifts morning after — all outlets publish at once
  • Event photography edited same night for next-day placement
Phase 3 · After

Echo

  • KOL content wave rolls out over 2 weeks
  • "Day in the life" film series inside the house
  • Founder interviews + industry-press deep dives
  • Awards submissions — architecture & product design

Website06

Two states: before and after the reveal.

State 1 · Teaser (now → event)

The closed door

  • Single dark page — the manifesto line and a countdown
  • Waitlist capture: "Request an invitation" form feeding the CRM
  • Private, passworded RSVP microsite for the 80 invitees
  • No product imagery — silhouettes and factory abstracts only
State 2 · Full site (embargo lift)

The open house — flipped live the morning after

Models — Mini A-01 specs & gallery Process — factory & precision story Sustainability — materials & carbon Journal — press, film, essays Inquire — consultation booking

Architecture-studio tone: full-bleed photography from the event shoot, restrained type, dark palette matching this document. Inquiry form routes to the same pipeline as event leads — one funnel, one CRM. Analytics, SEO basics, and Line OA linkage from day one.
