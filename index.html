The road to opening

The first house
meets its first guests.

The first house is standing on the Fameline lawn. What happens next decides how Mini A enters the world. This is the master plan for the private opening — every workstream from the exhibition guests walk through before touching the house, to the card that lands in their hands, to the story the press tells the morning after.

Opening · Friday 27 November 2026 · 18:00 Fameline Office Lawn, Bangkok ≈60 guests / evening · house tours of 4–5 Private, invitation-only evening First batch of exclusive customers
The first house — on site at Fameline lawn
The organizing idea01

One continuous journey, not an open house.

The house is the one thing that cannot scale: with more than 4–5 guests inside, the smart systems stop reading and responding the way they were designed to. So the evening is built as a rotation — intimate house rounds on a timed schedule, wrapped in a programme rich enough that no guest ever feels they are waiting.

Dinner runs all evening. Guests circulate — nobody stands in a waiting line.

Exhibition content02

Five zones before the front door.

A temporary pavilion / covered walk on the lawn, sequenced so each zone answers the next natural question: Why does this exist? → How is it made? → What is it made of? → How precise is it? → How does it live with me?

A Zone A · Manifesto

The Story Wall

A typographic wall stating the thesis: housing re-imagined as a manufactured product. Vision, the A49 × Fameline partnership, and the problem with conventional construction — told in three panels.

Produce — copywriting (TH/EN), large-format print, wall lighting.
B Zone B · Process

The Factory Film

A dark micro-theatre looping the 6–8 min documentary: robotic fabrication, QC gates, module assembly, the drive from factory to this exact lawn. Ends on a timelapse of the house landing on site.

Produce — film edit, seating for 12, sound isolation, projection.
C Zone C · Material

Material Library

Touchable samples: wall composites, glazing sections, floor systems, joinery. Each labelled with durability and maintenance data — the 10-year no-maintenance promise made physical.

Produce — sample plinths, spec cards, ambient light.
D Zone D · Precision

The 1:10 Model

A 1:10 scale cutaway model of the full house — structure, insulation layers, services routing and connection logic, open to view. Small enough to walk around, detailed enough to prove factory precision. Tolerances shown beside automotive comparisons.

Produce — 1:10 model fabrication, display plinth, annotation graphics.
E Zone E · Smart living

Living Intelligence

Interactive station previewing the aIoT layer: presence, climate preconditioning, security, energy view. A live feed from the actual house primes what guests are about to experience inside.

Produce — demo app build, screen station, live house link.

Invitation system04

The invitation is the first product they touch.

Physical format

An aluminium case, not a card

A brushed-aluminium hinged case, precision-folded in the same metal language as the house — fabricated with Fameline's own metalwork. Inside: the letterpress invitation and the guest's edition number. After the evening it lives on as their name-card case — a Mini A object that stays on the desk.

Brushed aluminium · precision hinge Reusable as a name-card case Numbered edition QR → private RSVP page Hand-delivered by courier
Digital companion + cadence

Three-touch sequence

T−6 wksSave-the-date — personal Line/email note from an A49 or Fameline principal. No details, just the date and "keep this evening."
T−4 wksThe box arrives — physical invitation, QR to private RSVP microsite with teaser film and logistics.
T−1 wkConcierge confirmation — personal call, dietary needs, arrival time, parking or car service arranged.
RSVPPrivate page: RSVP, +1 policy (spouse/partner), NDA-lite media note, calendar file.
PR strategy05

Whisper. Reveal. Echo.

The event is private, but its story is public. PR runs in three phases around the evening — scarcity before, spectacle during, momentum after.

Phase 1 · Before

Whisper

  • Teaser site + cryptic social presence (no product shots)
  • One anchor magazine granted an exclusive embargoed feature
  • Factory BTS clips seeded to architecture community
  • KOL scouting — 3–5 design/lifestyle creators signed
Phase 2 · Event week

Reveal

  • Press preview day — journalists tour before guests
  • Embargoed press kit: release, specs, imagery, founder bios
  • Embargo lifts morning after — all outlets publish at once
  • Event photography edited same night for next-day placement
Phase 3 · After

Echo

  • KOL content wave rolls out over 2 weeks
  • "Day in the life" film series inside the house
  • Founder interviews + industry-press deep dives
  • Awards submissions — architecture & product design
